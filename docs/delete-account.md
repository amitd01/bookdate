# Delete your BookDate account

You can permanently delete your BookDate account and all associated data at any time.

## In the app (instant)

1. Open **BookDate** and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account, profile, approximate location, swipes, matches and messages are deleted immediately.

## Without the app

Email **[amitdas+bookdatesupport@gmail.com](mailto:amitdas+bookdatesupport@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20BookDate%20account)** from the address you signed up with (or tell us your Apple "Hide My Email" address) with the subject **"Delete my BookDate account"**. We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted and what is kept

- **Deleted:** account and sign-in details, profile (name, birthday, gender, preferences, genres, bio), approximate location, push token, swipes, matches and all messages.
- **Kept for up to 12 months:** reports you filed or that were filed about you, only for safety and legal purposes. Encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.
