# BookDate Support

We're happy to help. Email **[amitdas+bookdatesupport@gmail.com](mailto:amitdas+bookdatesupport@gmail.com)** and we'll reply within 2 business days (safety reports within 24 hours).

## Common questions

**How does matching work?**
Swipe right on book covers you love (or find a book with the search button and tap the heart). When a reader within your chosen distance (up to 15 km or 10 miles) also likes the same book, and you both fit each other's preferences, it's a match and your book-club chat opens. One like can match you with several readers at once.

Every book you like is a standing invitation: if someone nearby likes it next week, you'll match then.

**What's "Just meeting people"?**
Friends mode. Choose it in onboarding or Profile → Looking for. You'll match with readers of any gender who are also here for friends; dating and friends readers never match each other. Switching modes keeps your existing matches, tagged Date or Friend.

**Can I browse other genres?**
Yes. Tap **For you** at the top of Discover and pick a genre. Your profile genres don't change, and it goes back to For you when you close the app.

**What does "A few readers nearby" mean?**
How many readers who fit your preferences opened BookDate nearby in the last 30 days. We show rough bands (just you, a few, 10+, 50+) so no one can be singled out.

**Why do you need my location?**
Only to find readers within the distance you chose (change it in Profile → Edit profile). It's rounded to about 100 m and never shown to anyone.

**How do I report, unmatch or block someone?**
Open the chat and tap the **shield** in the top-right corner.
- **Report** sends a private report (with optional details) to our team, which reviews it within 24 hours.
- **Unmatch & block** deletes the chat for both of you, and you'll never match or hear from each other again.

Reporting also unmatches and blocks. Either way your like on the book stays, so it can still match you with other readers.

**How do I change my preferences?**
Profile → Edit profile. Your birthday can't be changed in the app; if it's wrong, email us and we'll correct it.

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, swipes, matches and messages immediately.

**I'm not seeing any new books.**
Browse another genre, search for a book you love, or check back later. We add books regularly.

## Safety tips

- Keep chatting in BookDate until you feel comfortable. You never have to share your number.
- Meet in a busy public place (a bookshop or café is ideal), get there on your own, and tell a friend where you'll be.
- **BookDate will never ask you for money**, and a match shouldn't either. Report anyone who does.
- Trust your instincts. You can unmatch at any time, and they won't be told why.
- If you're in danger, call your local emergency number (112 in India).
